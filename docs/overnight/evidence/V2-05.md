## 1. Summary of Changes

### A. Telegraphed Hazards & Counter/Cost Rules (`src/game/entities/Obstacle.ts`, `src/game/systems/SpawnSystem.ts`)
- **Telegraph System:** Obstacles now display a 700ms warning telegraph banner prior to arrival at the chaser (`"⚠ লাফ দিন! (JUMP)"` for low hazards or `"⚠ লেন বদলান! (LANE)"` for solid barriers) with pulsing comic tween.
- **Classification & Cost:**
  - Low hazards (`pothole`, `brick`, `fake-wallet`): Drain 8 patience, 0.8x chaser speed multiplier for 1.2s. Countered by jumping (`chaser.isJumping`).
  - Medium obstacles (`barricade`, `shopping-bag`, `wire-coil`): Drain 12 patience, 0.7x speed multiplier for 1.5s.
  - Heavy hazards (`crowd`, `rickshaw`): Drain 15 patience, 0.65x speed multiplier for 1.8s.
- **Kick Counter:** In `CombatSystem.ts`, kick melee attack destroys obstacles directly ahead of the chaser within 140px, restoring +4 patience.
- **Fixed G-06 (Villain Trips):** Removed random 50% villain obstacle trip. The villain now vaults over street obstacles by default and **only** trips if stunned/lured by the player (`stateName === "HIT_STUN"` or `"TRIPPED"`).

### B. Archetype Counter-Moves & Defenses (`src/game/entities/Villain.ts`, `src/game/systems/CombatSystem.ts`)
- **Archetype Counter-Moves:**
  - `phoneSnatcher`: Sudden zig-zag lane shift + 180ms side-step roll dodge.
  - `pickpocket`: Drops fake-wallet trap behind him.
  - `cableChor`: Swings wire coil hazard in his lane.
  - `manholeChor`: Raises manhole cover as a shield to block 1 incoming projectile or melee hit.
  - `eidMarketChor`: Tosses shopping bag obstacles behind him.
  - `chickenChor`: Executes agile side-step dodge roll.
  - `chorSardar`: Summons an aggressive goon minion into an adjacent lane.
- **Dodge Mechanics:** CombatSystem checks villain `dodgeRate` (`rng.chance(villain.archetype.dodgeRate)`). On dodge, villain triggers a side-step roll, floats `"ডজ!"`, and negates incoming attack damage.

### C. Boss HP Scaling & Hidden Override Removal (`src/store/useGameStore.ts`, `src/game/scenes/RunScene.ts`)
- Added `isBossStage: boolean` and `setIsBossStage(bool)` in Zustand store.
- Updated `startGame()` to initialize `villainHp` and `villainMaxHp` using `villainConfig.baseHp` (scaled by 1.6x if `isBossStage` or archetype is `chorSardar`).
- Removed hidden boss override on 3rd run (`runCount % 3 === 0`) in `RunScene.ts`, ensuring boss stages are explicitly structured and deterministic.
